# Credits

Tall Grass Clicker is an unofficial fan game. The code is MIT (see `LICENSE`); the creatures are not ours.

## The creatures

The names, the 649 creatures of generations 1 to 5 and their artwork belong to **Nintendo, Creatures Inc. and GAME FREAK inc.** Pokémon and Pokémon character names are trademarks of Nintendo. This project is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by them; it is free, has no ads and takes no donations.

- **Pictures** (`docs/assets/sprites/`, `docs/assets/items/`, `docs/assets/badges/`): the Black & White animated sprites, the Generation VII icons, and the item icons and gym badges of the interface, from [PokeAPI/sprites](https://github.com/PokeAPI/sprites). Not covered by the MIT license, see `docs/assets/sprites/NOTICE.md`.
- **Data** (`data/` and `src/engine/dex.generated.ts`): names, types, base stats, evolution chains and habitats from the CSV files of [PokeAPI](https://github.com/PokeAPI/pokeapi) (BSD-3-Clause), turned into one table by `scripts/gen-dex.mjs`. The type chart is the well known 18-type one.
